How it works

Build here. Host anywhere.

1 · Build

Make it in the tab

Add your images or models and place your content. Your files stay on your device.

2 · Preview

Try it for real

The preview runs the actual tracker on your camera, so what you see is what visitors get.

3 · Export

Download a folder

One zip with the page and its assets. It works as-is, with nothing of ours left in the loop.

4 · Host & share

Put it online

Any host that serves HTTPS will do (the camera needs it): GitHub Pages, Netlify or your own site. Then make the QR.

A tool, not a platform

There is nothing for us to switch off.

Many AR services host your experience for you, then charge for views, for removing their watermark, or for keeping the QR code alive. Here there is no account to cancel and no redirect between your code and your visitors. The QR points at your URL, so it works for as long as your page does.

That also means we don't host it for you. If you'd rather have a project built and run for you, that's what Vértice Criativo does.
