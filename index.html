AR Studio

Compile an image target and preview a model. Everything runs in this tab — nothing is uploaded.
Both run entirely in this tab. Nothing you choose is uploaded.
Step 1

Image target

The thing a phone points at. Flat, matte and feature-rich tracks best — lots of distinct corners, not large flat areas. Around 1000 px on the long edge is plenty.

Step 2

Model

A .glb is the safe choice — one file, geometry and textures together. Keep it small: on a show floor it loads over congested Wi-Fi.

Step 3

Placement

Where the model sits relative to the target. The target is one unit wide, so 0 0 0 is its centre and scale 1 means roughly target-sized.

Step 4

Preview

Runs the real tracker with your compiled target and model. It will ask for the camera. Point it at the target — on screen works, but print tracks better.
